Ball return & dispensing · Issue 3 concept

13 holes, one funnel, one lift

Whichever of the 13 holes the ball drops into, it lands on the client's V-funnel under the 3° deck, rolls down the funnel's valley to its single outlet, and runs through a short tube to two solenoid gates. A vertical plunger then lifts it into a small hood. It rolls down a wedge into a tray, and the tray keeps up to five balls ready to putt. The lift stops when the tray is full.
